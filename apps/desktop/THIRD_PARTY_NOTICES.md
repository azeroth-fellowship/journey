## Rust (Tauri) dependencies

Several hundred crates (all platforms combined) are linked into the companion, listed in `apps/desktop/src-tauri/Cargo.lock`. License expressions found:
MIT, Apache-2.0, "MIT OR Apache-2.0", Unlicense, Zlib, ISC, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, 0BSD, CC0-1.0, Unicode-3.0,
CDLA-Permissive-2.0, MPL-2.0 and combinations of these.

Notes:

- Apache-2.0-only crates (for example `tao`, `sync_wrapper`) are compatible with GPL version 3, which is why the
  project is GPL-3.0-only and not GPL-2.0.
- MPL-2.0 crates (`cssparser`, `selectors`, `dtoa-short`, `option-ext`, ...) are file-level copyleft and are
  compatible with GPL-3.0 through MPL section 3.3. They are used unmodified.
- `r-efi` offers `LGPL-2.1-or-later` as one of three alternatives (the others are MIT and Apache-2.0); we use it
  under MIT/Apache-2.0. It is a UEFI-only dependency and is not part of the Windows build.
- No dependency is licensed under GPL-incompatible terms (no SSPL, BUSL, proprietary or "non-commercial" licenses
  were found).

Full license texts of each crate are in their source packages (`cargo vendor` or <https://crates.io>).
